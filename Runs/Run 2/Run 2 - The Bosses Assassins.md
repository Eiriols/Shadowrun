Yevgeny has received a contract for the protection Arend Valentin from a [[Zeta-ImpChem]] construction office. He's a medium level manager who's been targeted by several attacks and survived all by mostly luck. No information has been given why he's being targeted, the office is worried that internal comms are compromised and would be at risk if private security are hired.

Yevgeny assumes that the risk to Arend will be increased as the previous attempts have failed.
#### The Client
He's a medium level manager based in [[Z-IC Tegel]] who runs a office in charge of the production of more. He's known for being cruel to other staff members, often insulting and abusing them, callously firing them if they disagree or don't perform to his high standards. Much of his work involves finding ways to reduce costs without causing issues which often means blaming others for his decisions. 

"The Office" created the  contract after Arend was targeted by a series of assassination attempts that most recently involved blowing up his car. He now believes someone in his company was aiming to take take him out.
#### The Vory v Zakone
The Vory targeted Arend after he reneged on a deal with them on a new construction contract. The Vory saw this as a direct slight against them and got a low level member to take out tho they failed. They've now started to send small groups to wait outside his home to take him out when he next leaves. 
#### The Colleague
Reinhold Johannes also works in the same office and has often been overlooked for promotions and opportunities and thinks Arend is benefiting from nepotism.

He started off by hiring a small time hitman to poison Arend but after the attempt failed he hired a experienced assassin that's still attempting to kill the target. 
### The Run
This is the last attempt to take out Arend so both sides are going all out. It's been arranged for him to be transported to a private airport via two teams. The first is the team, the second will be meeting them at the end of a construction area that is being used by the company.
