Astral stuff, less decking, more driving, some decent combat (not bunched up)

[[Loci - Mage Spy|Loci]] brings a profitable job. Stealing a bunch of foci from a Wiz Gang called the [[Triglawer]]. They have heard that they are bringing a bunch of these foci from outside the city into a central operating point. 

They are brining them to their base in [[Falkensee]] from 3 different points:
1. [[Spandau]] in a small base of operations for the Dead Warlocks
2. An Corpo Distribution Centre in [[Schönefeld]]
3. Outside of [[Berlin - City|Berlin]] from the East, through [[Oranieburg]] & [[Aztech Schönewalde]]

#### 1 - Spandau
The Triglawers have taken over a small apartment building to run a protection racket on the local residents in the more recently unstable times of Spandau.

This location houses several mages as well as their friends and is rumoured by some to be a site of the gangs alleged organ harvesting and has been operating for several years.
#### 2 - Schönefeld
A medium size distribution centre with several private security. 

The main area is mostly operated by drones that move in complex automated patterns. They loaded with items in standardised featureless crates that are stored in the facility before being fetched and loaded into vans for deliveries or redistribution to other centres around the city. 
#### 3 - The Van
A Bulldog containing a supply run as well as the foci. It is meant to be arriving in 3-5 days but the exact date and time aren't known. Some details are known about the van and it's believed that it's yet to leave the [[Triglawer]] Base although which van it is is unknown. 
#### The Base
The Base is highly guarded by mages as well as a decent amount of tech. It's not known what the foci are being used and how long they'd be stored before being used by the gang. Any attempt to attack the base directly would be met with a firefight although taking out the base would be lucrative and beneficial to the people in the area; at least till someone else takes over.
