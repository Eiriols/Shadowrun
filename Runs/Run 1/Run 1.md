Test run for [[Main Friendly Faction]]

[[Yevgeny - Fixer|Yevegeny]] gathers in a soy-caf place in [[Lichtenberg]] team to ask them to do a small job, grab some data from rival drug runners.

They have a small warehouse in [[Gropiusstadt]] in which they're storing various drugs but the hit is for buyer data being stored in the warehouse, this data is actually for something more important (tbd).

the data is Air gapped on a data stick that's held in the bosses safe. This safe is in the office at the back of the warehouse.

Warehouse has a grid with security cameras, a few computers and a turret on it. The turret is running silent but has wifi enabled and is placed in the office and activates if there is someone in the room without the door being unlocked. 
#### Main Warehouse
A personnel door is located at the front. this requires a card reader to enter.

Shutters are on the right side, they can be opened from the inside with a switch or the outside with a number pad. 

Racks of materials and various items, some have drugs on them but the majority of are either materials or things to smuggle stuff in. 

There are 4 Gang Guards in the main room. (Corpo Sec - 1 Lieutenant )
