The JMWG are an socialist working group that supports many organisations that are looking to improve conditions for metahumans across Germany. This groups focusses on direct action, often action that is criminal and can be seen as immoral by other groups. This means they work with Shadowrunners often but lack a lot of support both financial and social. 

They operate across Germany but base themselves in [[Berlin - City|Berlin]] due to the anarchist support in the city.
