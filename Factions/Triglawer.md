The Triglawer are a Wizgang of neo-pagan orientation in the reunited [[Berlin - City|Berlin]] of the 2070s.
#### Activities
In addition to dozens of smaller gangs fighting for survival in [[Falkensee]], only a few have been able to survive over the years. One instance is the Triglawers, who in their self-portrayal celebrate themselves as left-green spirit liberators, but without further ado accept Aztech money in order to hunt down escaped critters from Aztech-Schönwalde.[2]

Regardless of the aforementioned nominal agenda, the Wizgang primarily deals with profit-driven magical crime, violent crime, and rioting. The main fields of activity are theft, robbery, trickery and the extortion of protection money using magic and spirits.

The wizard gang is also active in Talislegging and - in open contradiction to their official goal of fighting against the abuse of paracritters - also acts as critter hunters themselves. They sometimes sell their live prey through intermediaries to Aztechnology and its subsidiary, Trill AG, which run several critter farms in their corporate sector and in nearby Brandenburg. There are also rumours about blood magic practices in the inner circle around the leader Dragovit, and it is also said that the gang also practices organ legging and therefore maintains corresponding business contacts with Tamanous.
#### Members
The “Triglawer” gang consists of an estimated 40 magically gifted people who are said to use Sorbian magic, which is why hardly anyone would mess with them. However, it may be doubted whether they are really genuine Sorbs because since the beginning of the collaboration between Aztechnology and the Sorbian magician priests they have openly shown a connection to Aztech and act as its recipients.
#### Leader
The leader of the “Triglawer” calls himself Dragovit, which is obviously a cult or street name that underlines the neo-pagan orientation of the group and its devotion to pre-Christian, Slavic deities.
#### Territory
The territory of the “Triglawer” is in the alternative district of Falkensee, but they are not afraid to carry out their magical shenanigans far outside of their own turf, in the standard districts with tourist attractions, namely in [[Mitte]].
#### Allies
Regardless of their willingness to use violence, their neo-pagan orientation and the dark rumours that surround the Wizgang, they are considered allies of the Magnificent Lodge of the Holy Eris, which in turn originally emerged from the old Magnificans, and thus has very close connections to the Discordian movement.
